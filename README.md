# 🌐 Can You Really Build a Business Website for Free?

## A South African Business Guide to the Real Costs of Running a Professional Website

---

### 🧠 Introduction: Is a Free Website Really Free?

Starting a business often means watching every expense carefully.

So when you see a website builder offering a **free website**, it can be tempting to think:

> "Why pay someone to build a website when I can create one myself for free?"

The short answer is that **yes, you can create a website for free**.

But creating a website and running a professional business website are two very different things.

A free website can be useful for testing an idea, learning how websites work, creating a personal project or establishing a temporary online presence.

For a business that wants to attract customers, build credibility and become easier to find through Google, there are usually additional costs and considerations.

At **MyDailyLink**, we believe the better question is not simply:

> **"Can I build a website for free?"**

It is:

> **"What does my business need from its website?"**

---

## 💡 What Does a Free Website Usually Give You?

Free website platforms can provide many of the basic tools required to get a website online.

Depending on the platform, you may receive:

✅ A website editor  
✅ Website templates  
✅ Basic hosting  
✅ A platform-generated web address  
✅ Basic website functionality  

This can be enough for someone who simply needs a basic online page.

However, free plans can also come with limitations.

These may include:

❌ Platform-branded web addresses  
❌ Provider advertising or branding  
❌ Limited storage or bandwidth  
❌ Fewer design options  
❌ Limited business functionality  
❌ Restricted SEO features  
❌ Limited support  
❌ Restrictions on forms, integrations or ecommerce functionality  

For a personal project, these limitations may not matter.

For a business trying to attract customers online, they can become much more important.

---

## 🌍 Why Your Domain Name Matters

Your domain is the address people use to find your business online.

For example:

`yourbusiness.co.za`

Many free website platforms instead provide a subdomain that may look more like:

`yourbusiness.platformname.com`

Both can technically take visitors to a website.

But your domain is also part of your business identity.

A professional domain can make it easier for customers to remember your company and recognise your brand when they see it online.

For businesses targeting customers in South Africa, a **.co.za domain** can also help establish a clear local identity.

---

## 🚨 Don't Make the Mistake of Thinking a Website Is Just a Design

A website can look good and still fail to help a business.

Before building a website, think about what you want visitors to do.

Can they quickly understand what your business offers?

Can they find your services?

Can they easily contact you?

Can they navigate the website from their phone?

Can they find the information they need before making a decision?

Can they see why your business is relevant to them?

A professional business website should be designed around the customer journey rather than simply choosing an attractive template.

---

## 🔎 What About Google Search?

Having a website does **not automatically mean your business will appear at the top of Google**.

Search visibility depends on many factors.

These can include:

✅ Useful and relevant content  
✅ Website structure  
✅ Mobile usability  
✅ Technical performance  
✅ Relevance to search queries  
✅ Website content and information  
✅ Overall website authority  
✅ Local search relevance  

For example, a business operating in Boksburg may want to appear when potential customers search for services in Boksburg, the East Rand, Johannesburg or Gauteng.

This is where **website design and digital marketing can work together**.

Your website provides the foundation.

Content, optimization and digital marketing can help more potential customers discover that foundation.

## ⏰ The Hidden Cost of a Free Website

One of the easiest costs to overlook is **your own time**.

If you build the website yourself, you may need to spend time:

✅ Choosing a design  
✅ Creating pages  
✅ Writing content  
✅ Finding images  
✅ Configuring website settings  
✅ Testing forms  
✅ Updating information  
✅ Fixing problems  
✅ Maintaining the website  

That time could otherwise be spent on customers, sales, operations and growing your business.

So while a website builder may not charge you an initial development fee, your website can still have a real cost.

That cost may simply be measured in **your time and effort**.

## 🏗️ DIY Website vs Professional Website Design

There is nothing inherently wrong with building a website yourself.

A DIY website can be a practical solution when:

✅ You are testing a new business idea  
✅ You are learning about websites  
✅ You need a temporary landing page  
✅ You are creating a personal project  
✅ You do not currently require advanced functionality  

But businesses often become more demanding as they grow.

You may eventually need more pages, better customer journeys, improved search visibility, product catalogues, social media integration or ongoing website management.

A professional website solution can help structure these elements around the actual needs of the business.

## 🚩 Questions to Ask Before Choosing a Website Solution

Before deciding how to build your business website, ask yourself:

### 1. What do I actually need my website to accomplish?

Is it simply an information page, or should it generate enquiries and support sales?

### 2. Who are my customers?

Where are they located, and what are they likely to search for online?

### 3. Do I need a professional domain?

Will customers recognise and remember the website address?

### 4. Will the website work properly on mobile?

A website that only looks good on desktop is not enough.

### 5. How will people find the website?

Will you rely on Google, social media, marketplaces, advertising or a combination?

### 6. Who will maintain the website?

Someone needs to update information as your business changes.

### 7. What will the website cost over time?

Do not only consider the initial setup cost.

Think about hosting, maintenance, content, marketing and future improvements.
